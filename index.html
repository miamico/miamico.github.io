Research focus

I study how probabilistic hardware and stochastic algorithms shape each other in thermodynamic computing. My current work spans hardware calibration and control, parametrized stochastic circuits, and the compilation of stochastic programs to native thermodynamic dynamics.

My earlier research focused on quantum algorithms, error suppression and mitigation, quantum control, and non-stationary cavity quantum electrodynamics.
